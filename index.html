What layering sounds like

A binaural voice straddles its carrier: the left ear gets half the beat below it, the right ear half above. The two ears never hear the beat itself, only the two tones, and the beat appears where they meet. Stack five voices and the session has a texture no single tone can produce: the thing Brainwave Generator users came for, rebuilt with every value on screen.
